# Security Policy

Elitze Agentic Platforms — maintained by Terrell Hall (Founder/CEO).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **security@elitze.ai** with a description, reproduction steps, affected
versions or endpoints, and any proof-of-concept. Please do not open a public
issue for security reports.

Machine-readable contact: [`/.well-known/security.txt`](public/.well-known/security.txt)

## Response targets

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement | 1 business day |
| Triage and severity assignment | 3 business days |
| Status update cadence | Every 5 business days |
| Critical fix or mitigation | 7 days |
| High fix | 30 days |
| Medium / low fix | 90 days |

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against good-faith research that respects user
privacy, avoids service degradation, uses only accounts you own or have
permission to test, and gives us reasonable time to remediate before public
disclosure.

## Out of scope

Missing best-practice headers with no demonstrated impact, volumetric denial of
service, social engineering of staff or customers, findings from automated
scanners without a working proof-of-concept, and issues in third-party services
we do not control.

## Supported versions

The deployed `main` branch is the supported version. Dependencies are scanned
continuously and patched on the schedule above.
